## Strongest contributions

- **Attempt 01**: Best polished end-to-end appendix structure, especially definitions for canonical/base canonical/profile evidence and a balanced treatment of `meta.profile`, SMART Health Cards, and QuestionnaireResponse.
- **Attempt 02**: Best concise FHIR-version framing and selector-to-evidence table, including clear single-resource vs. Bundle traversal guidance and test-writer notes.
- **Attempt 03**: Best examples and comparison table contrasting raw FHIR JSON with SMART Health Card Artifacts, plus strong warnings that `profilesFrom[]` is not a package descriptor or URN.

- **Attempt 05**: Best privacy/minimization tone for broad family requests, strongest Bundle inspection checklist, and best phrasing that signed SMART Health Cards can be valid yet not selector-responsive.

## Contradictions, missing pieces, and overreach

- **Normative scope of Appendix H**: Some attempts used conformance language that could read like new request/response requirements. I kept Appendix H subordinate to §§5-6, following the dependency instruction that Appendix H supports those sections and must not introduce new requirements silently (`spec.md.outline.dependency_tree` lines 195-203; `spec-work/t2a-clinical-request-model/orchestrator-review.md` lines 37-39; `spec-work/t2b-clinical-response-model/orchestrator-review.md` lines 34-36).
- **FHIR R4 vs. later releases**: Attempts varied between “FHIR R4 only” and multi-release examples. I described R4 as the idiom and example base while preserving §5 `fhirVersions[]` and §6 Artifact `fhirVersion` behavior. Accepted §5 says `fhirVersions[]` can include release strings such as `4.0.1`, `4.3.0`, and `5.0.0` (`spec-work/t2a-clinical-request-model/canonical.md` lines 86-93), while §6 requires raw FHIR Artifacts to declare `fhirVersion` (`spec-work/t2b-clinical-response-model/canonical.md` lines 138-155).
- **`profilesFrom[]` value space**: Attempts agreed on profile-family semantics but differed on whether to mention package ids, URNs, and registries. I preserved the accepted rule that `profilesFrom[]` is an array of canonical profile-family URLs and not a package descriptor, implementation-guide object, package id, registry alias, or local topic vocabulary (`spec-work/t2a-clinical-request-model/canonical.md` lines 296-305). Active code validates a non-empty array of canonical URLs (`rp-web/src/sdk/core.ts` lines 137-173), and tests reject string/object forms (`rp-web/src/protocol/index.test.ts` lines 82-111).
- **Profile selector additivity vs. `resourceTypes[]`**: All attempts identified additivity, but some risked saying `resourceTypes[]` narrows profiles in a way that could blur the distinct constraints. I preserved §5: `profiles[]` and `profilesFrom[]` are additive, while `resourceTypes[]` is a separate resource-type constraint (`spec-work/t2a-clinical-request-model/canonical.md` lines 306-323). Active docs say exact profiles do not limit the broader `profilesFrom` request (`docs/SMART-HEALTH-CHECKIN-REQUEST-RESPONSE.md` lines 279-297).
- **Canonical `|version` handling**: Attempts converged on preserve-for-claims and strip-for-fetch/routing, but differed in detail. I used the accepted §5.5 decision matrix, including preserving `QuestionnaireResponse.questionnaire` and returned `meta.profile`, stripping for fetch/profile-family lookup, and comparing at the same normalization level (`spec-work/t2a-clinical-request-model/canonical.md` lines 500-523). Active documentation has the same matrix (`docs/SMART-HEALTH-CHECKIN-REQUEST-RESPONSE.md` lines 455-470), and Android tests confirm stripping a Questionnaire canonical before fetch (`wallet-android/app/src/test/java/org/smarthealthit/checkin/wallet/SmartRequestAdapterTest.kt` lines 59-65).
- **Raw FHIR JSON Artifact shape**: Attempts agreed that `application/fhir+json` carries a single Resource or Bundle and requires outer `fhirVersion`; I retained this as a restatement of §6. Active TypeScript types require `fhirVersion` for raw FHIR Artifacts (`rp-web/src/sdk/core.ts` lines 59-75), active validation checks it (`rp-web/src/sdk/core.ts` lines 243-246), and docs show the same Artifact shape (`docs/SMART-HEALTH-CHECKIN-REQUEST-RESPONSE.md` lines 803-821).
- **Mixed-version Bundle handling**: Attempts varied between SHALL-level prohibition and practical detection caveats. I restated §6: all resources in one raw FHIR Artifact are interpreted under the Artifact `fhirVersion`; Wallets/Responders do not mix releases in one raw FHIR Artifact; Verifiers reject or quarantine detected mixed-version Bundles. I also kept the caveat that detection can be conservative because FHIR resource bodies may not self-label release versions (`spec-work/t2b-clinical-response-model/canonical.md` lines 372-379).
- **SMART Health Card FHIR layer**: Attempts agreed that SMART Health Card Artifacts have no outer `fhirVersion`, but some text risked treating the wrapper as FHIR content. I kept the accepted rule that `application/smart-health-card` uses `value.verifiableCredential[]`, has no outer `fhirVersion`, and requires inspection of signed payloads for FHIR version and profile evidence (`spec-work/t2b-clinical-response-model/canonical.md` lines 111-122). Active tests reject an outer `fhirVersion` on SMART Health Card Artifacts (`rp-web/src/protocol/index.test.ts` lines 134-159).
- **QuestionnaireResponse construction and validation**: Attempts aligned on canonical, inline, and combined forms, but differed on whether to require `QuestionnaireResponse.questionnaire` for inline-only cases. I preserved §5.5 and §6.6: preserve the requested canonical including `|version` when that canonical is the identity being answered and known; inline-only cases can derive from `Questionnaire.url` and `Questionnaire.version` when safe; material canonical/resource disagreement maps to `unsupported` before answering and `error` for later operational failure (`spec-work/t2a-clinical-request-model/canonical.md` lines 369-458; `spec-work/t2b-clinical-response-model/canonical.md` lines 281-292 and 380-387).
- **Full FHIR profile validation**: Attempts differed on Verifier obligations. I did not require full FHIR validation because §5 allows matching from `meta.profile` or equivalent trusted evidence and §6 says absence of `meta.profile` is not automatically a protocol error (`spec-work/t2a-clinical-request-model/canonical.md` lines 288-295; `spec-work/t2b-clinical-response-model/canonical.md` lines 380-388). Appendix H recommends preserving and inspecting evidence while separating protocol validation from downstream clinical acceptance.
- **US Core/CARIN examples**: Attempts used US Core and CARIN examples consistently. I kept them explicitly illustrative, matching T1's rule that examples do not imply endorsement or certification status (`spec-work/t1a-editorial-terminology/canonical.md` lines 13-20) and §5's illustrative examples (`spec-work/t2a-clinical-request-model/canonical.md` lines 330-367).
- **Overreach avoided**: I did not define mdoc, kiosk, JSON Schema, registry mechanics, EHR write-back, universal Questionnaire rendering/SDC behavior, FHIR package resolution, Artifact profile-summary fields, or full-profile-validation requirements. Those belong to other sections, deployment profiles, or local policy.

## Resolutions with repository evidence

- Preserved the transport-neutral clinical boundary from T1/T2: SMART requests and responses have the same clinical meaning across same-device, kiosk, and future transports (`spec-work/t1b-purpose-scope-goals/canonical.md` lines 7-18; `spec-work/t1c-architecture-roles-flows/canonical.md` lines 22-35).
- Grounded selector fields in active code: `profiles[]`, `profilesFrom[]`, `resourceTypes[]`, and questionnaire canonical/resource forms are the active TypeScript model (`rp-web/src/sdk/core.ts` lines 11-27), and active documentation describes no-selector behavior, additivity, and resource-type narrowing (`docs/SMART-HEALTH-CHECKIN-REQUEST-RESPONSE.md` lines 240-327).
- Grounded canonical `|version` handling in accepted §5 and active docs: preserve for `QuestionnaireResponse.questionnaire`, returned `meta.profile`, fixtures, and exact conformance; strip for fetch, profile-family membership, and routing (`spec-work/t2a-clinical-request-model/canonical.md` lines 500-523; `docs/SMART-HEALTH-CHECKIN-REQUEST-RESPONSE.md` lines 455-470).
- Grounded response Artifact mapping in accepted §6 and active implementation: core media types are `application/smart-health-card` and `application/fhir+json`; SMART Health Card Artifacts use `value.verifiableCredential[]`; raw FHIR JSON Artifacts use `value` plus `fhirVersion` (`rp-web/src/sdk/core.ts` lines 4-7 and 59-75; `spec-work/t2b-clinical-response-model/canonical.md` lines 109-155).
- Grounded Bundle and `meta.profile` guidance in active docs and accepted §6: raw FHIR Bundle resources are interpreted under the Artifact `fhirVersion`, Artifacts should not include a profile summary field, and Verifiers inspect `value.meta.profile` or `Bundle.entry[].resource.meta.profile` (`docs/SMART-HEALTH-CHECKIN-REQUEST-RESPONSE.md` lines 821-828 and 1098-1106; `spec-work/t2b-clinical-response-model/canonical.md` lines 380-385).
- Grounded no-selector and broad family examples in tests and fixtures: Android parses a broad `fhir.resources` item with no selectors, routes versioned profile canonicals, and handles `profilesFrom[]` arrays (`wallet-android/app/src/test/java/org/smarthealthit/checkin/wallet/SmartRequestAdapterTest.kt` lines 26-57); fixture vectors include US Core, CARIN, and questionnaire examples (`wallet-android/app/src/test/resources/test-vectors.json` lines 12-30).

## Downstream decisions

- Appendix B should encode raw FHIR vs. SMART Health Card Artifact shape rules where JSON Schema can express them, but leave Bundle traversal, profile-family membership, `QuestionnaireResponse.questionnaire` comparison, and full FHIR profile validation to procedural conformance tests or deployment profiles.
- Appendix A should index normative requirements from §§5-6 and any deliberately final Appendix H requirements, not every explanatory sentence in this appendix.
- Worked examples should include raw FHIR single Resource, raw FHIR Bundle, SMART Health Card, exact-profile, profile-family, resource-type, no-selector, and QuestionnaireResponse cases, while keeping US Core and CARIN illustrative.
- Fixture vectors should preserve canonical strings and `meta.profile` values exactly, including `|version` suffixes where present.
- Future registry or deployment-profile work may define machine-readable profile-family maps, media-type compatibility rules, stronger FHIR validation requirements, or explicit IG/package sources for `profilesFrom[]` evaluation.

## Open issues

- No blocking open issues for T2.C canonical text.
- Mixed-FHIR-release detection inside arbitrary Bundles remains inherently limited when resources do not self-identify their release; conformance tests should focus on wrapper `fhirVersion` behavior and clear conflict cases.
- Final examples still need a publication-wide decision on specific US Core/CARIN versioned versus unversioned canonicals.
