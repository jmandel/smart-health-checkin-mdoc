# Appendix A. Conformance checklist

This appendix indexes testable obligations defined elsewhere in SMART Health Check-in 1.0. It does not create independent requirements. Optional-feature rows apply only to implementations that claim the feature or to deployment profiles that make the feature mandatory.

| ID | Target | Level | Section | Checklist item | Evidence/validation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A-001 | Requester/Verifier | SHALL | §4.1.1 | Identify claimed target, feature/profile, specification version, and deployment profile in each conformance claim. | Claim document lists target(s), profile label(s), optional features, version, and deployment-policy dependencies. |
| A-002 | Requester/Verifier | SHALL | §4.1.1 | Keep requester identity, origin, reader credentials, kiosk metadata, callbacks, and trust assertions out of the SMART request body. | Inspect generated requests and extension fields; verify trust evidence appears only in transport/deployment layers. |
| A-003 | Holder Wallet/Responder | SHALL | §4.1.2 | Validate SMART requests under §5 before response construction and preserve item ids for `fulfills[]` and `requestStatus[].item`. | Negative malformed-request tests; response references exactly original item ids. |

## Organizer notes

### Strengths

- The checklist is an index of accepted obligations from §§4-14 and Appendices B-D; it does not introduce new protocol behavior.
- Rows are split by conformance target, one stable section reference, requirement level, and a testable validation hook.
- Stale `requestProfile`, preset, IPS shortcut, “all of the above,” dynamic-element, and inline-§8 kiosk patterns appear only as negative checks.
- Optional profiles are explicit: direct `org-iso-mdoc`, kiosk, readerAuth, schema/CDDL/fixtures, provider behavior, and deployment/profile authoring.

### Caveats

- Some SHOULD/MAY rows are inherently policy-review or UX-review checks rather than pure byte-level tests.
- Appendix B cannot express several cross-object constraints, so procedural validation remains mandatory.
- Appendix D currently identifies no checked-in deterministic `fixtures/kiosk/` suite; kiosk fixture rows are future-vector expectations, not evidence of existing vectors.

### Open issues

- Decide whether final Appendix A should remain this detailed or be split into core, mdoc, kiosk, security/privacy, registry, and fixture subsections.
- Confirm whether zero-item requests remain allowed (`items[]` required but not `minItems: 1`) or become a hard negative conformance case.
- Decide how final fixture profiles will encode comparison mode and trust labels for real-platform captures versus deterministic generated vectors.

### Downstream dependencies

- T6.C should align the fixture index and generated vectors with these row IDs or the final renumbered Appendix A rows.
- T7.D should verify that all section numbers remain stable and that registry labels in §§4 and 13 match the final publication text.
